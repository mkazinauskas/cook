## Ingredients

- 500g bread flour
- 325g water
- 10g salt
- 2.5g active dry yeast, or 6g fresh/pressed yeast
- 15g olive oil

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the water (proof active dry yeast 5-10 min until foamy; fresh yeast just needs stirring).
2. Whisk the flour and salt in a bowl, add the yeast water, and mix until no dry flour remains.
3. Knead 8-10 minutes until smooth, working in the olive oil near the end.
4. Shape into a ball, cover, and rise at room temperature for 2 hours, until doubled.
5. Divide into portions, ball tightly, and refrigerate 24 hours to 3 days.
6. Bring to room temperature 1-2 hours before baking. Stretch, top, and bake at the highest oven temperature until golden and blistered.
