## Ingredients

- 600g 00 flour (at least 12g protein)
- 390g water
- 17g salt
- 1.2g active dry yeast, or 4g fresh/pressed yeast

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the water (proof active dry yeast 5-10 min until foamy; fresh yeast just needs stirring).
2. Whisk the flour and salt in a bowl, add the yeast water, and mix until no dry flour remains.
3. Knead 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. Shape into a ball, cover, and rise at room temperature for 2 hours, until doubled.
5. Divide into portions, ball tightly, and refrigerate 24 hours to 3 days.
6. Bring to room temperature 1-2 hours before baking. Stretch by hand (avoid a rolling pin, to keep the air in the rim), top lightly, and bake as hot as your oven or wood-fired oven will go until puffed and leopard-spotted.
