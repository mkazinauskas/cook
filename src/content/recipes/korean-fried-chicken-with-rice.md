## Ingredients

- 800 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-sized pieces
- 5 g salt
- 120 g potato starch (or cornstarch)
- 500 ml neutral oil, for frying
- 45 g gochujang
- 30 ml soy sauce
- 40 g honey
- 15 ml rice vinegar
- 10 ml sesame oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 600 g cooked rice
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 10 g sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken with salt, then toss in potato starch until coated.
2. Heat the oil to 180°C. Fry the chicken in batches for 6-7 minutes, until golden and cooked through. Drain on a wire rack.
3. Whisk the gochujang, soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and garlic in a pan. Warm over medium heat for 2 minutes.
4. Add the fried chicken and toss to coat in the glaze.
5. Serve over rice, topped with spring onions and sesame seeds.
