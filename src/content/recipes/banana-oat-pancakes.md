## Ingredients

- 2 ripe bananas
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of salt
- Butter or oil, for the pan

## Instructions

1. Add the bananas, eggs, oats, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt to a blender. Blend until smooth.
2. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes so the oats can absorb some liquid.
3. Heat a lightly greased nonstick pan over medium-low heat.
4. Pour small rounds of batter onto the pan and cook for 2-3 minutes per side, until golden and set.
5. Serve with fruit, yogurt, or maple syrup.
