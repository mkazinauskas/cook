## Ingredients

- 60 ml (3 jiggers) vanilla vodka
- 80 ml (4 jiggers) passion fruit purée (or pulp of 2 passion fruit)
- 40 ml (2 jiggers) passion fruit liqueur (e.g. Passoã)
- 40 ml (2 jiggers) vanilla syrup
- chilled prosecco, to taste, to serve

## Instructions

1. Add the vodka, passion fruit liqueur, vanilla syrup, and passion fruit purée to a cocktail shaker filled with ice.
2. Shake hard for about 15 seconds, until well chilled.
3. Double strain into a chilled martini glass.
4. Serve immediately alongside a shot of chilled prosecco, poured or topped up to taste.
