## Ingredients

- 225 g udon noodles (3/4 of a 300 g pack)
- 500 g chicken thigh meat
- 150 g onion
- 150 g red pepper
- 150 g carrot
- 3-5 cloves garlic
- neutral oil, for the wok

### Sauce

- 3 eggs
- 60 g soy sauce
- 80 g teriyaki sauce
- 1-2 tbsp peanut butter
- 10 g sriracha (optional)

### To finish

- sesame seeds
- peanuts
- 50-100 g spring onions, green parts

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken and vegetables into medium slices ahead of time. Mince the garlic as finely as possible and slice the spring onions for scattering.
2. Boil the noodles 1-2 minutes short of the time on the pack, then cool them under cold water so they do not stick.
3. Whisk the sauce ingredients together until smooth.
4. Heat the kamado and the kazan to 200-230°C. Pour in the oil and let it heat through so nothing catches.
5. Add the chicken and cook for 2-3 minutes. Do not stir much, or it will steam instead of sear.
6. Add the onion and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until it turns slightly translucent.
7. Add the carrot and red pepper and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly.
8. Add the garlic for a moment only, just long enough to warm it through.
9. Add the noodles and pour in the sauce. Toss everything for 1-2 minutes until it comes together.
10. Take the wok off the heat, plate up, and scatter with sesame seeds, peanuts and spring onions.
