## Ingredients

- 2 carrots, cut into batons
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F) and line a large sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Toss all the vegetables with olive oil, thyme, oregano, salt, and pepper in a large bowl.
3. Spread the vegetables in a single layer on the sheet pan, making sure not to overcrowd.
4. Roast for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until caramelized and tender.
5. Serve warm as a side, or over grains for a full meal.
