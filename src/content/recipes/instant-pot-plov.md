## Ingredients

- 80 ml olive oil
- 0.7 kg lamb shoulder (or beef chuck), cut into 2.5 cm chunks
- 2 large onions, halved and sliced
- 4 large carrots, cut into thick matchsticks
- 400 g long-grain rice (such as basmati), rinsed until water runs clear
- 600 ml water or broth
- 1 whole head of garlic, outer papery skin removed, top trimmed
- 6g cumin seeds
- 2g coriander seeds
- 2g dried barberries or raisins (optional)
- 14g salt
- 1g black pepper

## Instructions

1. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté (high). Add the oil and heat until shimmering. Brown the lamb in batches, about 5 minutes per batch. Remove and set aside.
2. Add the onions to the pot. Cook, stirring occasionally, until golden, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the carrots. Cook for 3 more minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Return the lamb to the pot. Stir in the cumin, coriander, salt, and pepper.
5. Pour in 350 ml of the water or broth. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom. Cancel Sauté.
6. Seal the lid and set the valve to sealing. Pressure Cook on high for 30 minutes to tenderize the meat.
7. Let the pressure release naturally for 15 minutes. Release any remaining pressure and open the lid.
8. Spread the rinsed rice over the meat and vegetables. Don't stir. Pour the remaining 250 ml of water or broth evenly over the rice.
9. Press the garlic head, root-side up, into the center of the rice. Scatter the barberries or raisins on top, if using.
10. Reseal the lid and set the valve to sealing. Pressure Cook on high for 5 minutes.
11. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes. Release any remaining pressure.
12. Remove the garlic head. Fluff the rice gently with a fork, mixing in the meat and carrots.
13. Serve the plov on a large platter, topped with the whole garlic head for guests to pull apart.
